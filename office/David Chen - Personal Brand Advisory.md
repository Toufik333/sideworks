# Personal Brand Advisory
### Executive Social Media Playbook

*Prepared for: David Chen, EVP & Head of Human Resources | Advisor: Toufik*

---

## 1. The Goal

Build your personal brand as a credible, visible voice in HR and leadership — without adding meaningful time to your week. The strategy: batch content creation in short bursts, post consistently, and let format variety (not volume) do the work of building an audience.

---

## 2. Where He Should Show Up

| Platform | Priority | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Primary — ~90% of effort | His core professional audience: HR peers, execs, recruiters, media. All post types below live here first. |
| **X (Twitter)** | Optional / secondary | Only if he wants broader public and media visibility. Shortened or threaded versions of LinkedIn content — not new writing. |

---

## 3. Content Formats (What to Post)

Variety of format matters more than volume. These seven types cover nearly everything a credible HR/leadership voice needs:

| Format | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Contrarian take** | Push back on conventional wisdom | Sparks debate; signals independent thinking |
| **Practical / listy advice** | Numbered or bulleted how-to from experience | Easy to skim, save, and share |
| **Opinion / provocative** | Clear stance on a buzzword or trend | Builds a recognizable, consistent POV |
| **Story / case study** | A real situation, beginning to end | Most authentic; concrete beats abstract |
| **Behind-the-curtain** | What a decision actually looked like internally | Humanizes leadership; rarely shown publicly |
| **Reaction / commentary** | His take on news, a study, or a viral post | Keeps him timely; fast to produce |
| **Personal reflection** | A mistake or hard-won lesson | Vulnerability often outperforms polish |

## 4. Beyond Text Posts: Video, Images & Engagement

Text posts are the foundation, but the following add reach and depth with minimal extra time:

### A. Short-form video

- Talking-head clips (30-90 sec) work best — David restating one idea from a text post directly to camera, filmed on a phone. No editing skill needed.
- Turn one written post into a script: the post's first line becomes the hook, the bullets become the spoken points.
- Native platform video (posted directly to LinkedIn/X) outperforms links to YouTube — keep it in-app.
- A quarterly "talking head" batch session (record 4-6 short clips in one sitting) covers a full quarter of video content.

### B. Images and graphics

- Quote cards: pull the sharpest line from a text post and turn it into a simple branded graphic (useful for restating a post's core idea visually).
- Behind-the-scenes photos: a whiteboard from a meeting, a team offsite, a hallway conversation — low effort, high authenticity.
- Simple data visuals: one stat from an internal decision (e.g. the engagement-score change from the anchor-days example) turned into a one-chart image.
- These can be generated in bulk alongside the text drafts, using the same source material — no separate creative process needed.

### C. Commenting on other people's content

This is one of the highest-leverage, lowest-effort activities for building visibility — often more efficient than original posting, since it borrows someone else's audience.

- On articles: comment with a specific reaction or a related experience from his own work — never just "Great read!" A strong comment reads like a mini-post (2-4 sentences, one clear point).
- On other people's LinkedIn posts or videos: agree-and-extend ("This matches what we saw when..."), respectfully disagree with a reason, or add a data point/example they didn't cover.
- Target 3-5 thoughtful comments per week on posts from peers, industry figures, or company leadership — this keeps him visible between his own posts and costs under 10 minutes.
- Comments on his own posts (replying to people who engage) should also get a response within the first hour or two — this is what LinkedIn's algorithm rewards most, and it's a 5-minute daily habit, not a content-creation task.

---

## 5. Summary: A Realistic Weekly Time Budget

| Activity | Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Batch content session (voice/text) | 15-20 min | Every 3-4 weeks |
| Reviewing/editing drafts | 10-15 min | Weekly |
| Posting (copy-paste) | 2-3 min per post | 2-3x weekly |
| Commenting on others' content | 8-10 min | Weekly |
| Quarterly video batch | 30-45 min | Every 3 months |

*Total ongoing time: roughly 30-40 minutes a week, plus one slightly longer session every month — a realistic ask even for a fully booked EVP calendar.*
