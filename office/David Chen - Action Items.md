# What to Ask David Chen to Do
### Action Items for the Personal Brand Advisory

## Before We Start (One-Time Setup)

- [ ] **Optimize your LinkedIn profile** — Update headline, About section, and banner to reflect your HR/leadership voice (I can help draft these if you send me your current profile)
- [ ] **Pick 5–10 people to follow and comment on** — HR peers, industry figures, company leadership whose posts you'll engage with regularly
- [ ] **Decide on X (Twitter):** Are you in or out? If yes, I'll handle cross-posting shortened versions — no extra work on your end

---

## Monthly: Batch Content Session (~15–20 min)

- [ ] **Block 20 minutes once every 3–4 weeks** for a voice-note or text dump session with me
- [ ] **Come prepared with 2–3 raw ideas** from any of these prompts:
  - A decision you made recently and why
  - Something you disagree with that most HR people accept
  - A mistake or hard lesson from your career
  - A behind-the-scenes moment (a tough meeting, a policy change, a team conversation)
  - Your reaction to something you read or saw in the news
- [ ] **Just talk or type freely** — I'll turn the raw material into polished posts across all seven formats (contrarian take, practical advice, opinion, story, behind-the-curtain, reaction, personal reflection)

---

## Weekly: Review & Post (~15–20 min total)

- [ ] **Review the 2–3 draft posts I send you each week** — approve, tweak, or reject (~10–15 min)
- [ ] **Copy-paste and post the approved drafts** to LinkedIn (and X if applicable) — (~2–3 min per post)
- [ ] **Reply to comments on your posts within the first 1–2 hours** — even a short, genuine reply is fine (~5 min daily habit)

---

## Weekly: Commenting on Others' Content (~8–10 min)

- [ ] **Leave 3–5 thoughtful comments per week** on posts from your target list of people
- [ ] **Each comment should be 2–4 sentences with one clear point** — agree-and-extend, respectfully disagree, or add a data point/example
  - ❌ Never just "Great read!" or "Love this!"
  - ✅ "This matches what we saw when we rolled out anchor days — engagement scores jumped 12% in the first quarter."

---

## Quarterly: Video Batch Session (~30–45 min)

- [ ] **Block 30–45 minutes once every 3 months** to record 4–6 short talking-head clips on your phone
- [ ] **Each clip = 30–90 seconds**, restating one idea from a text post directly to camera
- [ ] **I'll send you a script for each clip beforehand** (the post's hook becomes your opening line, the bullets become your talking points)
- [ ] **Film in decent lighting, phone propped up, no editing needed** — send me the raw clips and I'll handle the rest

---

## What I Need From You to Make This Work

| What I Need | Why | How Often |
|---|---|---|
| 20 min voice/text dump | Raw material for all your posts | Every 3–4 weeks |
| 10–15 min draft review | Your voice, your approval | Weekly |
| Post the approved content | Keeps it authentic (posted from your account) | 2–3x/week |
| Reply to comments | Algorithm boost + relationship building | Daily (5 min) |
| 3–5 comments on others' posts | Visibility between your own posts | Weekly (8–10 min) |
| 30–45 min video recording | Quarterly video content in one sitting | Every 3 months |
| Decision on X (Twitter) | So I know whether to cross-post | One-time |
| List of 5–10 people to engage with | Target list for commenting strategy | One-time (update as needed) |

---

*Total ask: ~30–40 minutes a week + one monthly batch session + one quarterly video session.*
*I handle everything else: writing, formatting, graphics, scheduling strategy, and iteration.*
